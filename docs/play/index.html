Decay

Notes with a life: they sit at temperature, they grow with attention, and they compost into a soil you can still search.

about

Write a note

Leave the firing time empty for a plain note. Give it a number of days and it becomes an idea that must sit at temperature: locked until it cools, and it can accrue additions meanwhile.

Firing

Waiting is the feature. While a note is firing you can add to it, but you cannot act on it.

Nothing is firing.

Living

Size, weight and opacity follow vitality: reopening and linking raise it, neglect lowers it. Click a note to reopen it (that is attention), then link it to another.

Nothing living yet. Write a note above.

Search everything

Living notes and composted ones, in one result list. A word that only a composted note holds is still findable — that is why the soil exists instead of a delete button.

The soil

Composted notes keep their links. Re-seeding brings one back into the living view with those links intact.

The soil is empty. Notes fall here when their vitality drops below the rot threshold.
Decay keeps one colony on this device: nothing is deleted, nothing is uploaded, and no model guesses your links — you make them.
